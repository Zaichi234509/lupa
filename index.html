Her Story

Born to the roar of the crowd

She fights with the heart of a wolf.

Born Lupa Silva of the once-noble House Silva, she abandoned her aristocratic ties to walk the path of the blade. Now a non-congenital Resonator of Septimont, she has never once tasted defeat in the arena.

Her name means “she-wolf” in an ancient tongue — and it fits like a gauntlet. Fiercely protective of her city, relentlessly competitive in battle, fiercely loyal to those who earn her respect. When her Broadblade ignites, the roar of the crowd becomes a hymn to her victory.
