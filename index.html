Who is Lupa?

Born Lupa Silva of the once-noble House Silva, she abandoned her aristocratic ties to pursue the path of combat. Now a non-congenital Resonator of Septimont, she has never tasted defeat in the arena.

Her name — "she-wolf" in ancient tongue — speaks to her nature: fiercely protective of her city, relentlessly competitive in battle, and fiercely loyal to those who earn her respect. When the flames of her Broadblade ignite, the roar of the crowd becomes a hymn to her victory.
